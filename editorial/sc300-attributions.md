# SC300 attribution decisions

SC300 follows modern, publicly recognizable author attributions when the
pinned Wikisource revision differs from the mainstream attribution. The
original source attribution remains recorded here and in
`source-locks/sc300.json`; no poem text is changed by these decisions.

all historical witnesses agree.
